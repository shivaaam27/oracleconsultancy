01

What this system is

Part A — Orientation

This is a single Chief-of-Staff command centre — one website that runs the day-to-day operations of Oracle Consultancy and its portfolio. It replaces a sprawling Excel workbook with a living system that captures work, tracks people and compliance, remembers meetings, and helps you make decisions.

The problem it replaces

Before this system, operations lived in spreadsheets: tasks, staff details, documents, leave, and notes were scattered and went stale. The command centre brings it all into one place with a memory — every task has a history, every person has a profile and compliance checklist, every meeting is saved, and the whole portfolio can be glanced at on a single brief.

It provides:

  • Task capture and tracking, with a per-task timeline and a portfolio-wide activity feed;
  • Company and portfolio risk views;
  • Saved meeting notes, minutes and AI-assisted meeting intelligence;
  • A full HR & admin operating system (people, documents, assets, leave, organogram);
  • Per-person reminder drafts and outreach;
  • ORI, the built-in assistant (Oracle Intelligence), and voice dictation.

Getting in — logging on & security

Part A — Orientation

The admin side sits behind one owner password; staff have their own personal portal logins. Sign-in is one tabbed screen.

The single login screen (/login)

One screen, two tabs:

  • Staff Login (the default tab) — staff sign in with an identifier (name or email) and their personal password.
  • Command Centre — the owner signs in with the owner password.

If you set an owner identity (name/email in Settings), it becomes a required second factor on the Command Centre tab. Leave it blank and it stays password-only (no lockout risk).

Passkeys — Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, fingerprint

Both the owner and staff can register a passkey so they sign in with their face or fingerprint instead of typing a password. The system stores only the public key — never your biometrics.

  • Owner: register in Settings.
  • Staff: register in the portal profile ("Sign in faster").
  • The login screen offers a passkey button and autofill.
Two cookies, two worldsThe admin side uses the cos_admin session; the portal uses cos_portal. They are separate — signing out of one does not affect the other.

What's protected

An edge "gate" checks every admin page before it loads. Staff portal pages are gated separately and only ever show a person their own data. Directors can view across companies but still cannot reach the owner-only admin tools.

Good habitSet an owner identity and register a passkey on your main device. It makes the Command Centre both faster to enter and harder for anyone else to enter.

Finding your way around

Part A — Orientation

There is no menu bar. Everything is reached from one floating pill at the bottom of the screen — on phone, tablet and desktop alike.

The navigation pill

A single bottom-floating pill carries the main destinations and the page action. Its tabs:

Home Director Brief Task Management Workbook Chat HRMS + (page action) Search Theme

Chat is a primary tab on both the admin and the staff-portal pill, so messaging is always one tap away.

The HRMS launcher

The HRMS icon opens one centred "Go to" launcher listing every secondary destination, so you never hunt for a page:

Tax & LegalOrganogramOECR (stock) Assets & VendorsLeave & AttendanceOCR (cleaning) CompaniesPeopleDocuments Letters & LetterheadsOutboxInbox InsightsSettings

Search & the “+” action

  • Search opens a fast command surface — type to jump to a task, person, company or page.
  • “+” is the action for the page you're on (e.g. on Tasks it creates a task).
  • Theme toggles light/dark.
One look everywhere — “Aurora”The whole system shares one design language called Aurora: calm liquid-glass panels, one cool-blue accent, centred layouts, iPhone-style toggles, gentle motion. Every page, pop-up and form looks and behaves the same way, so once you learn one screen you know them all.

Guided tours

The system has built-in onboarding tours — first-run guided walkthroughs and feature spotlights that introduce new buttons and panels once per person. Staff can replay them from their portal profile. New features self-guide automatically, so the system teaches itself as it grows.

Tasks & the command centre

Part B — Module quick reference

The home command centre (/) has three views: Overview, Companies and Tasks. Tasks are the heartbeat of the system.

Where

Home / → Tasks tab · new task at /task/new · a task opens as a drawer (/?tab=tasks&task=CODE).

Creating & tracking a task

  • Press + (or "New task"). Give it a title, company, category, priority and assignees.
  • The task gets a code like DS-001 automatically.
  • Open any task to see four tabs: Overview, Conversation (post updates), History (full audit trail), and Edit.
  • On a phone the task drawer opens full-screen; swipe actions are available on rows.

Statuses, priorities & categories

StatusesNot Started · In Progress · Under Review · Blocked · Waiting External · Escalated · Completed · Closed. “Open” = anything except Completed/Closed.
Priority / RiskCritical · High · Medium · Low
CategoriesFinance · Operations · Marketing · HR · Legal · Technology · Sales · Admin · Meetings · Strategy · Other

Timeline & activity feed

Every change is recorded. Each task has its own timeline; the Home Timeline view shows a portfolio-wide activity feed across all companies. Nothing is silently lost — deleting/closing is tracked.

Every number is a doorThroughout the system, counts and figures are clickable — tap a "3 overdue" figure to see exactly which three. Observe, then act.

People & HR records

Part B — Module quick reference

Every staff member, expat, outsider and candidate has a rich record: HR profile, documents & compliance, leave, assets, onboarding journey, reporting lines and history.

Where

/people (directory) · open a person for the cockpit drawer · printable form at /people/form.

The person record

  • Profile: department, role, start date, date of birth, nationality, IDs, address, emergency contact, probation end, and work site + residence (shared locations list).
  • Staff IDs are computed live (e.g. CZ-E04, OC-D02) from company prefix + role letter — not stored.
  • Person types: local staff · expat · outsider · candidate. Each type drives its own document checklist.
  • Drawer tabs: hero tiles (status at a glance) + Details, Leave, Manage, History.

Lifecycle automation

EventWhat happens automatically
New staff addedAn onboarding checklist + a document-compliance checklist are created.
Person archivedAn offboarding checklist is created and assigned assets are auto-returned.
Probation approachingConfirm passed / extend / create a review task from the drawer.
Field changedLogged to the person's History (who/what/when).

Reporting lines & the organogram

  • Primary manager is on the person; "also reports to" lines are secondary.
  • Cards show manager + number of direct reports; the drawer lists direct reports.
  • The full picture is the Organogram (see §8) — a portfolio flowchart with multi-parent reporting.
  • You can set "also reports to" for several people at once via the bulk select bar in the directory.

Smart fill & the paper form

You can drop text or files onto a person and the system fills blanks only (it never overwrites, and always asks you to review). For staff with no system access, print the bilingual EN/Swahili form at /people/form (a field agent fills it by hand, you photograph it, and intake builds the profile).

Companies & reference data

Part B — Module quick reference

The Companies hub is the reference-data centre — the master lists everything else hangs off.

Where

/companies hub (tabs) · /companies/[id] for one company.

The hub tabs

Four lists, each with add / rename / merge / delete:

CompaniesThe seven companies + their branding/letterhead details (legal name, address, TIN, logo, signatory…).
DepartmentsDepartments and per-company department heads.
SitesShared locations where staff live/work (not company branches).
RolesManaged job-title list. Renaming/merging re-points everyone using it.

The company profile

/companies/[id] has Overview, Profile, Tasks, Timeline and Org. The company profile also carries the board-level governance data (cap table, beneficial owners, key persons, signatories, resolutions, risks, decisions) — kept out of the daily/weekly views but available for transfer packs.

Merge, don't duplicateIf two departments or roles mean the same thing, use merge — it re-points every person and record to the surviving entry, so you never end up with split data.
07

Documents & compliance

Part B — Module quick reference

One place for every document, wired to a per-person and per-company compliance checklist that scores to 100%.

Where

/documents · smart intake at /inbox.

How compliance works

  • Each person type (and company) has a requirement profile → a checklist of required documents.
  • When you upload a document, the system auto-links it to the matching requirement by category/title.
  • You verify each item and can set a "valid until" review date; items lapse automatically when expired or stale (>180 days).
  • The score updates everywhere immediately — person drawer, staff portal, and the Director Brief.

Uploading & tidying

ActionHow
Single docAdd via the full document form (owner = a person or a company).
Many at once"Add several" — bulk multi-file upload, each reviewed in turn.
DuplicatesRecency-aware detection: "Keep both" or "Replace + archive".
CompilationsA multi-doc PDF is detected and can be split into linked parts.
RenewalsReplacing a doc hands the checklist tick from the old to the new one.

Smart intake (Inbox)

Drop pasted text and/or files into /inbox as a bundle, then press Process: the review queue files the documents to the right owner and enriches the person profile (blanks only, always reviewed). Compliance recomputes automatically.

Chase gaps in one clickNeeds-attention rows have a "Chase" button that drafts a renewal reminder to the person or company in the Outbox — de-duplicated so you don't nag twice.

The HRMS suite

Part B — Module quick reference

A full HR & admin operating system, built on the principle reuse, don't duplicate. Reached from the HRMS launcher.

Tax & Legal (/hrms/command-centre)

Recurring tax / statutory / legal obligations with cadence and a safety net so nothing slips.

Organogram (/hrms/org)

The portfolio view is a layered flowchart: multi-parent reporting, role/seniority tiers, company shown as colour, shared-service roles in-flow (primary boss = solid line, extra bosses = dashed). Also per-company trees, a by-department view, and an "Everyone" web.

Assets & Vendors (/hrms/assets)

  • Assets: durable equipment assigned to a person, or shared to a company with a custodian. Auto-returned on offboarding. Per-asset history, PDF register export, handover receipts, retire/restore.
  • Vendors: suppliers, contractors and landlords; their contracts live as documents.

Leave & Attendance (/hrms/leave)

Two tabs, grounded in the Tanzania Employment & Labour Relations Act 2004:

LeaveTypes, requests, approvals and balances. Entitlements: Annual 28/12mo · Sick 126 days/36mo (63 full + 63 half) · Maternity 84 · Paternity 3 · Compassionate 4. Working days are Mon–Sat minus public holidays.
AttendanceA month grid you brush-to-paint (Present/Absent/On leave/Holiday/Remote/Half-day/Sick), filter by company, "mark all Present today". On-leave/holiday days auto-fill. Staff self-check-in from the portal.

Applications in progress (/hrms/pipeline)

A kanban of in-flight bureaucracy (permits/visas/licences): To Apply → Applied → Control No. Issued → Paid → Receipt Received → Issued. Attach a supporting document at any stage.

Commitments register (/hrms/registers)

Leases, insurance and commercial contracts. The system computes notice-by = end date − notice days and flags when notice is due soon.

Stock & cleaning

  • OECR (/hrms/oecr) — Office Equipment Control Registry: consumable stock control (items, purchases, issues).
  • OCR (/hrms/ocr) — Office Cleaning Registry: a daily cleaning checklist.

Meetings & the workbook

Part B — Module quick reference

The Meeting Workspace turns meetings into saved business memory; the Workbook holds meetings, notes and to-dos together.

Where

Meeting Workspace /meeting · Workbook /workbook.

The Meeting Workspace can

  • Save title, company, date, attendees, raw notes and minutes
  • Take voice dictation into notes
  • Clean rough notes
  • Generate minutes
  • Extract decisions
  • Extract risks
  • Draft a follow-up
  • Extract action items → bulk-create tasks

Workbook

Letters & the Director Brief

Part B — Module quick reference

Director Brief (/brief)

A glanceable portfolio report: headcount, compliance, risks, an HR section, completed/closed this month, statutory obligations and leave liability.

  • Share via WhatsApp / Email / Copy, or print to PDF (detailed per-company tables are print-only).
  • Directors get a "Download PDF" button on their portal board.

Letters (/letters)

System-wide branded PDF letters with a Draft → Issue lifecycle.

  • Per-company letterhead set on the Letterheads tab: typed fields, a designed header+footer image, or a full-page A4 background.
  • Draft → Issue freezes a letterhead snapshot and stamps a reference (PREFIX/INV/YYYY/NNN) — reprints are identical.
  • Full body editing; PDF via in-place print; optional Outbox draft; no auto-send.
  • First template is the Invitation letter (auto-pulls invitee name, nationality, passport, DOB, role).
Adding a new letter typeIt's a small code change: add an entry to LETTER_TEMPLATES and a buildBody function in src/lib/letters.ts.

Chat & outreach

Part B — Module quick reference

Chat (/chat · portal twin /portal/chat)

Free-standing messaging, separate from task updates: direct messages and ad-hoc groups, everyone-to-everyone.

  • WhatsApp-style messenger: full-screen on mobile, two-pane glass card on desktop.
  • Optimistic send, read receipts, typing indicator, inline image previews.
  • Real-time delivery (with a polling fallback). Notifications deep-link into the thread.

Outbox & reminders (/outbox)

Persisted message drafts — per-person reminders, renewal chases, director messages. Nothing sends automatically; each draft gives you a WhatsApp / email / SMS deep-link to send yourself.

Email & WhatsApp automation

ChannelStatus
EmailBuilt and wired (Gmail SMTP / Resend). Inert until provider credentials are set in the environment; directors can then be made to send "as themselves".
WhatsAppSandbox-proven on Twilio, not yet live. Today the system produces wa.me deep-links (you tap send). Automated sending needs Twilio business verification, an approved number and message templates, then three environment variables.
Owner action to go liveBoth email and WhatsApp are wired but inert until you add the provider credentials in Vercel. See the project memory notes (emailwork, director_surface_plan) for the exact steps.

The Staff Portal

Part B — Module quick reference

The portal is a first-class surface for staff — same Aurora look as the admin side, but it only ever shows a person their own data.

Where

/portal · sign in at /portal/login · profile at /portal/profile.

What staff can do

  • See only their own tasks, post updates, and make limited status moves (never Completed/Closed).
  • Your documents — see their compliance checklist and upload onto gaps.
  • Your attendance — self check-in with a week strip; a check-in pop-up auto-opens once a day.
  • Your leave — view balances and request leave.
  • Onboarding journey, assigned equipment, and passkey setup.

Portal roles

A person's portal role is set in Settings → Staff portal access. There are four:

staffOwn tasks, documents, attendance, leave and profile only.
managerStaff view + Team attendance today, Leave to approve, a team roster (compliance/onboarding scores), and can create tasks for their company.
hrGroup-wide task visibility and creation across all companies (no single-company limit).
directorThe board brief across all companies; create tasks group-wide, schedule events/meetings, and draft messages. Auto-routed to the board on sign-in.

Accessibility & comfort

The portal profile carries accessibility controls — text size, reduced motion and density — so staff can tune the experience on their own device.

Parity ruleThe portal keeps pace with the admin side automatically: shared styles, motion and components flow to it. It deliberately drops anything that would expose other people's data (the command surface, ORI, admin drawers).
Data is never deletedRevoking access, changing a role, or archiving a person keeps all their data. Access control and data are separate.

Intelligence — ORI, voice & search

Part B — Module quick reference

ORI — the assistant (Oracle Intelligence)

"Ask COS / ORI" answers questions over your real data — tasks, meetings, documents and people — and cites what it used (task codes, meeting title + date). It never invents data.

  • Recall is synonym-aware and relevance-ranked, with an honest "N tasks · M meetings" count.
  • There is an AI master switch; when AI is off, features degrade gracefully.

Voice intelligence

Voice is a shared layer, not just a microphone button. The pattern is "speak rough, save polished" — you dictate and the system tidies it up.

  • Used in meeting notes, quick capture and task updates.
  • Languages: English (en-GB), Swahili (sw-TZ), Hindi (hi-IN), Gujarati (gu-IN).
  • You can teach ORI names and phrases into a voice dictionary for better accuracy.

Smart / semantic search

Beyond keyword search, the system has a hybrid search that understands meaning (it indexes tasks, documents, meetings and people, blending full-text and vector search). It is opt-in — off by default; it needs a one-time setup (deploy the embedding function and run the backfill — see SEMANTIC_SEARCH.md) and is then toggled on in Settings. It is strongest in English; reliable multilingual support (Swahili/Hindi/Gujarati) is a pending cost decision (see appendix).

An "operating brain", not magicThe intelligence is grounded: it works from your records and cites them. If something isn't in the system, ORI won't pretend it is.

Settings, backups & day-to-day operations

Part B — Module quick reference

Settings (/settings)

Organised into icon-tile cards with a section nav. Key things you control here:

  • Owner identity (the optional second factor) and owner passkeys.
  • Staff portal access — set/reset a person's password, change their role, revoke access.
  • AI master switch and "high quality AI" toggle; voice language and dictionary.
  • Director outreach pause/resume (a governance kill-switch).

Technical appendix

Part C — For whoever maintains the system

Technology stack

LayerTechnology
FrameworkNext.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript 5
DatabaseSupabase Postgres via the pooler (port 6543); Drizzle ORM 0.45 + postgres.js
StylingTailwind v4 tokens from globals.css; the "Aurora" design kit

Critical configuration — do not break

  • src/db/index.ts must keep prepare:false and max:1 (required for PgBouncer transaction mode).
  • DATABASE_URL uses the Supabase pooler on port 6543.
  • The admin edge auth gate lives in src/proxy.ts (the Next-16 proxy convention, formerly middleware.ts). Its secret() derivation must stay identical to src/lib/admin-auth.ts and src/lib/portal-auth.ts.
  • All wall-clock columns are timestamptz; writes use .toISOString() (UTC), times render in the viewer's local zone (Dar es Salaam, UTC+3).
  • Never rm -rf .next while the dev server is running (it corrupts the build cache).

Schema areas (high level)

Core: companies, departments, people, tasks (+ assignees, updates). Reference: sites, job_titles, department_heads, reporting_lines. Meetings: meetings, meeting_tasks. Governance/transfer-pack: facts ledger, cap_table, beneficial_owners, key_persons, signatories, resolutions, risks, decisions, pipeline, commitments. HR: requirement_profiles/items, person_requirements, assets, vendors, leave_types, leave_requests, attendance, public_holidays. Documents: documents, inbox, document_links. Letters: letters. Stock/cleaning: stock_*, cleaning_*. Outreach: reminders, outbox. Chat: chat_threads/participants/messages/mentions. Auth: webauthn_credentials. System: audit_log, corrections, settings, notifications, system_events, daily_snapshots, undo_tokens. Full detail in memory/database_schema.md.
