# Studio — the Oracle design system

**Every page in Oracle is Studio** (since 24–26 Sept 2026). The owner showed the
Superpower health app and asked for Oracle to look and work like it: a big
title, two equal summary cards, then full-width rows or the record. **Every new
page, dialog, pop-up or panel is built in Studio without being asked.**

- **The mockup is the specification**: `design/studio-mockup/` (README there;
  `boards/*.dc.html`, generated by `gen/p_*.py` — read those for exact sizes).
  Compare a page at the boards' own size, **1440×900**, section by section,
  before calling it done. Phone boards are `M_*`, staff boards `S_*`.
- The running log, decisions and traps: `memory/studio_redesign.md`.
- **What it replaced.** *Desk* (the flat, grey, ERPNext-shaped language of Aug
  2026) survives only as **building blocks** Studio reuses — see §12. The desk
  sidebar, the floating nav pill and Aurora (glass) are **gone**; the per-page
  "New look" switches (`ui.studioPages`, `lib/studio.ts`) are gone too.

## 0. Studio at a glance

1. **Title, two cards, then the work.** A 56px title with its filters, two equal
   summary cards (one usually dark), then full-width rows or the record.
2. **Warm grey page, white rows, dark cards.** Page `#F3F3F1`, surface `#FFFFFF`,
   card `#141517`. Rows are separate white cards on the grey, not lines in a box.
3. **Ink is the action colour, not blue.** The primary button is black
   (`--st-ink`). Colour means something: **pink = late**, **amber = due soon**,
   **green = on track/done**, violet and blue for information only.
4. **Soft and round.** Cards 20px, buttons 11px, chips 10px, pills 8px. Circles
   stay circles — faces, dots, rings, arcs.
5. **Its own type scale**, fixed whatever the density: 11 · 12 · 13 · 14 · 15px,
   big numbers and titles above that (§3).
6. **Every number is a door**, and every attention list is **worst first**.
7. **It fits the frame.** From `lg` a page of columns needs no page scroll —
   each column scrolls inside itself (`useFitFrame`). Only a LIST scrolls.
8. **Readable text**: dark grey on white, light grey on dark — never mid-grey.
9. **Calm motion**, and none at all under reduced motion (§8).
10. **One set of screens for everyone.** Owner, directors, managers and staff use
    the same Home, Tasks, task page, People, Companies and Calendar; the DATA is
    cut on the server, not the look.
11. **Saving does not change.** A Studio screen calls the same actions and cores
    (`createTaskCore`, `updateTaskCore` as a PATCH) as before. Never a second
    writer.

## 2. Tokens

Everything sits inside **`<StudioScope>`** (a `div.studio`), which switches the
tokens on. **Colours come from `var(--st-…)`, never a hex in a page** — that is
what keeps dark mode working.

```
             light      dark
--st-page    #F3F3F1    #0E0F10     the grey field
--st-surface #FFFFFF    #17181B     rows, light cards, fields
--st-ink     #111214    #F2F2F0     text AND the primary button
--st-sub     #55585E    #D0D2D6     secondary text
--st-muted   #676A70    #B4B7BC     quiet text (lifted automatically on dark cards)
--st-line    #E4E4E0    #2A2C30     hairlines between things
--st-line-soft #F0F0EC  #222428     hairlines inside a card
--st-field-line #CFCFC9 #4A4D53     the edge of anything you type in
--st-card    #141517    #1C1D21     the dark card  (+ --st-card-2/-3, --st-card-line)
--st-on-card #F2F2F0                text on a dark card (--st-on-card-muted #D4D6DA)
--st-late #E0479E  --st-soon #F5A524  --st-ok #19C37D   marks (dots, bars, rings)
--st-late-text / --st-soon-text / --st-ok-text          the same, as readable text
--st-blue #2490EF  --st-violet #8B5CF6                  information only
```

Also: washes and edges (`--st-bad-line`, `--st-bad-wash`, `--st-warn-wash`,
`--st-ok-wash`), form bits (`--st-label`, `--st-dash`, `--st-scrim`,
`--st-track-off`, `--st-seg`), and the calendar set (`--st-cal-*`).

**The sheet set, `.st-sheet`** — the theme-following colours of every pop-up that
rises from the footer (Go-to, "+ New", search, notifications, `StudioSheet`):
`--sh-bg / fg / sub / muted / field / field-line / card / hover / line /
chip-line / on-bg / on-fg / pink-*`, light and `.dark .st-sheet`. Add the dotted
texture with `.st-sheet-dots`.

**Frame variables**: `--foot-h` (footer height, 64px), `--foot-safe` (what is
left of the iPhone home-bar inset), `--page-foot`, `--page-top`. **Every "above
the footer" offset is `calc(var(--foot-h) + var(--foot-safe) + …)`**, never a
literal.

**Desk's tokens are redefined inside Studio** (`.studio`, `[data-studio-foot]`,
`[data-studio-goto]`, and — while a Studio page shows — every direct child of
`<body>`, where pop-ups portal): warm greys, **`--accent` becomes ink**, softer
radii, Geist. That is why `FluidSelect`, `Combobox`, `DatePopover`, `RecordList`
and the rest render in Studio's look with no fork. **Do not fork a Desk control
to restyle it** — fix the token.

⚠️ Traps in the cascade:
- `.studio { color: var(--st-ink) }` is **unlayered** — a dark surface that
  itself carries `studio` needs an inline `color`, or its text is dark on dark.
- globals.css's unlayered `* { border-color }` and `input { … }` beat Tailwind
  utilities. Studio surfaces are excluded from the border rule; a **pop-over
  portalled to `<body>` must carry `studio`, `data-studio-foot` or
  `data-studio-goto`**, or its borders go Desk grey. A light input on a dark
  surface needs inline style.
- **Do not use Tailwind `dark:` for Studio colours** — it does not follow the
  `.dark` class here. Use a token defined under `.studio` and `.dark .studio`.

## 3. Type, shape, texture

- **Fonts**: Geist (body), Geist Mono via `.st-mono` for times, codes and
  figures. Loaded in `layout.tsx` as CSS variables.
- **Scale** (`.studio` pins `text-xs/sm/base` to 12 / 13 / 14px, ignoring
  Compact): meta 11px · labels 12px · **body 13px** · emphasis 14px · card
  titles 15px · figures 22/26/30px · big numbers 76px (`BigNumber`) · page
  title **34px phone / 56px from `sm`**. Use these sizes; do not invent one in
  between.
- **Radii**: card 20px · button 11px · chip 10px · on-card button 9px · pill 8px ·
  form field 10px · sheets up to 28px. `.studio .rounded-full` is a real circle
  (Desk squared it; Studio does not).
- **Textures** on cards: `st-tex-rings | contour | hatch | dots` (dark) and
  `st-tex-paper-dots | paper-rings` (light) — pass `texture` to `StudioCard`.
- **Quiet scrollbars** inside a card: `.st-scroll`.

## 4. The page pattern

```tsx
<StudioScope>
  <StudioHeader title="Tasks" left={<>…filter chips</>} right={<>…buttons</>} />
  <StudioCardRow>
    <StudioCard tone="dark" texture="rings"><CardHead label="Overview" />…</StudioCard>
    <StudioCard tone="light">…</StudioCard>
  </StudioCardRow>
  …the list or the record…
</StudioScope>
```

**Kit** (`kit.tsx`): `StudioScope`, `StudioHeader` (`title`, `left`, `right`,
`sub`), `StudioCardRow` (`cols` 2|3), `StudioCard` (`tone`, `texture`),
`CardHead`, `BigNumber`, `Ring` (0–100), `Arc` (half gauge), `Dot`,
`StudioPill` (`onCard` on dark), and the button looks **`stBtn`** — `dark`
(primary, 36px), `ghost` (hairline, 36px), `chip` (header filter, 32px),
`onCard` / `onCardGhost` (on a dark card, 32px).

- **Header**: the title is `data-page-header`. Filters sit beside the title
  (`left`), page actions on the right. **One primary (dark) button per header.**
- **Card row**: two equal cards; the dark one leads. Every figure on a card
  links to the list it counts. Cards scroll inside themselves rather than grow.
- **Rows**: `RecordList variant="studio"` — same engine (sorting, columns,
  bulk, export, keyboard, "where you were"), Studio looks: rows as white cards,
  tick box in the left margin (shown on hover or once anything is ticked),
  status as dot + word, faces, deadline as coloured words. `activeKey` rings the
  row shown elsewhere; `lead` draws a row first (the quick-add line).
- **Floating search / filter bar**: **always `stFloatBar`** — `sticky` for a bar
  in the page's flow, `fixedLg` when it lives in a panel that stops short of the
  footer, `page` when the whole window scrolls. It sits 12px above the footer;
  never hand-write a `bottom-…`.
- **Side panel** (Tasks' `TaskPanel`): a click on a row opens a 400px panel on
  the right, not a modal — the list stays live, another row swaps it, ×/Esc/the
  same row closes it. From `xl` the page makes room
  (`:has([data-task-panel]) main { padding-right: 440px }`); below that it
  floats; on a phone it is a sheet above the footer. ↗ grows it into the full
  page (`st-panel-grow`). A row's title links to the full record.
- **Record page**: a **band** (back link, code, the actions — dark band on a
  task), the name, pills/chips, **tabs**, then columns (details · conversation ·
  people) that **fit the frame** from `lg`/`xl`. Details **edit in place**: click
  a value, it becomes its own control (`StudioChoiceMenu`, date, people), saved
  through the PATCH core with an Undo toast. Tabs switch with
  `history.replaceState` (a router navigation re-reads the whole record).
  Secondary actions go behind ⋯. A record is a page with its own URL; the back
  link carries `?back=` (`BackLink`, `ReturnLink`, `lib/return-to.ts`).
- **An older Desk panel inside a Studio card**: wrap it in `.st-desk` (greys,
  hairlines, sentence case, ink buttons); `.st-panel` also dissolves its outer
  box. Reuse this before rewriting a panel.
- **Footer line**: a page may set the footer's note with
  `useStudioFootNote({ label, text, href, tone })` — tied to the path, so it
  never lingers.

## 5. The frame and the footer

`StudioShell` draws the frame and the footer on every signed-in page — owner,
director, manager and staff alike.

- ⚠️ **The rounded frame is an illusion.** The document still scrolls; a fixed,
  click-through `.studio-frame` paints everything outside a rounded rectangle
  with a huge box-shadow. Making `main` a scroll box would break every "back to
  where you were" (they read `window.scrollY`). Do not "fix" this.
- Everything keys off **`body:has([data-studio-frame])`**: page colour, `main`'s
  padding, `--page-foot`/`--page-top`, and sticky bars tagged `data-sticky-top`,
  `data-sticky-foot` or `data-savebar` clearing the frame.
- **Desk footer** (dark): next deadline · Home · **‹ page ›** (scroll or swipe
  steps through pages; neighbours prefetched) · Profile/Settings · Ask or search
  ⌘K · bell · **+ New**. Sign-out from `lg`.
- **Go-to panel** (`[data-studio-goto]`, the page pill): every page, grouped,
  type to filter, Enter goes; also Profile, sign-out and the ONE light/dark
  toggle. A selected item is inverted, so its icon tile is a tint of its own
  text colour.
- **+ New** opens the one card with a tab per record (Task, Note, Event, Person,
  Document, Company, Announcement), opening on the tab that fits the page. A page
  opens a tab with `window.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent("studio:new", { detail:
  { tab } }))`. "Open the full …" turns it into the record page as a draft.
- **Search** is one ranked list (Ask ORI first when the text reads as a
  question). **Notifications** is a theme-following panel with Needs you /
  Activity and Open · Reply · Mark read · Dismiss per row.
- **Who sees what**: `studioStops` (owner), `directorStops` (directors and
  managers — Profile instead of Settings, no everything-search), `staffStops`
  (staff — pages under `/portal/*`, "+" makes a to-do). Every portal page wears
  the Studio frame the portal layout draws; links inside shared screens go through
  `useStudioPaths()` so staff land on `/portal/*`.

## 6. Forms and pick-lists

- **A form is `.st-form`; a text box is `.st-field`.** `.st-field` gives the
  sheet's field colour and a visible `--sh-field-line` edge; `.st-form` lifts
  the Desk controls inside it (`FluidSelect`, `Combobox`, the date button) to
  the same **40px, 10px-radius** box. **Wrap a `DateInput` in
  `<span className="st-date block">`** so it matches.
- **Every text box has a visible edge.** A bordered Studio box that is, or
  directly holds, an input or textarea takes `--st-field-line` automatically —
  the card hairline is too faint to read as "type here".
- Labels are quiet 12px sentences: Desk labels carry `data-field-label` and
  Studio drops their capitals.
- **Pick-lists**: `StudioChoiceMenu` is THE in-place choice (status, priority,
  risk, category, company) — a dot-and-word trigger and a portalled card kept on
  screen. `StudioMenu` is the header chip that opens a list of links
  ("All companies ▾"), searchable, can open upwards. People: `StudioPeoplePick`.
  Dates: `DateInput` / `DatePopover` — never `<input type="date">`.
- Help text becomes a tooltip on a dense form; the save bar sits at the top of
  a record's Edit tab, or at the foot of a sheet. Settings uses one save dock
  (`settings-save-dock.tsx`) that appears only when a form has changed.
- ⚠️ **Never define a component inside a component's render** — it remounts on
  every keystroke and the field loses focus.

## 7. Sheets, pop-overs and menus

- **`StudioSheet`** is the pop-up for a form on a Studio page: it rises from the
  footer (or `centred` from `sm`), wears `.st-sheet` with dots, closes on Esc or
  a click outside. The Report (`report-sheet.tsx`) is one.
- **`BottomSheet`** remains for the few old portal and Desk forms; do not use it
  on a new Studio page.
- **`[data-st-menu]`**: tag every new dropdown. Below 640px it becomes a
  full-width panel along the foot of the screen, above the footer, never off the
  edge.
- **Escape closes one layer at a time.** A menu claims the key
  (`preventDefault`); sheets listen on `window`, so a menu inside always gets
  first refusal. Check `!e.defaultPrevented` before closing.
- Anchored menus portal through `useAnchoredMenu()` (`lib/use-anchored-menu.ts`)
  with `MENU_Z`, flip up when there is no room, and test outside clicks with
  `isInside()`. Do not hand-roll another.
- A tooltip or pop-over on a dark background that is NOT `.studio` must set its
  own text colour (the unlayered ink rule again).

## 8. Motion

- Classes in `globals.css`: `st-pop` (fade up 4px), `st-rise` (bars grow),
  `st-draw` (rings and arcs draw in; set `--st-len`), `st-slide-l/-r` (card
  slides), `st-panel-in` (side panel: from the right on a desk, from below on a
  phone), `st-panel-grow` (panel → full page), `st-face-peek` (task-row faces).
- 120–240ms, opacity and small translations. Subtask ticks, faces breathing and
  the footer pill are the livelier exceptions, and still stop for reduced motion.
- **Reduced motion both ways**: every Studio animation is switched off under
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` **and** `html[data-motion="reduced"]` (the
  manual toggle). A new animation needs BOTH rules. `Reveal` (`reveal.tsx`)
  already checks both — use it for entrances; do not hand-roll `motion.*`.
- ⚠️ `st-pop` animates `transform`, so an element placed with a translate loses
  its offset — place it with `top/bottom` instead.

## 9. Dark mode

- `next-themes` sets `.dark` on `<html>`; `.dark .studio` redefines the `--st-*`
  set, `.dark .st-sheet` the `--sh-*` set, and the Desk tokens follow. The frame
  page is `#121315`.
- Dark cards stay dark in both themes (a lighter `#1C1D21` at night). Anything
  drawn on `--st-card` gets light `--st-muted`/`--st-sub` automatically.
- The one theme toggle lives in the Go-to panel (and Settings → Appearance).
- To look at dark mode in the preview, emulate the colour scheme; the pane
  otherwise forces light.

## 10. Phone and tablet

Phone = below `md` (768px; mockup iPhone 393×852), tablet = `md`, desk = `lg`
(iPad landscape counts as desk). Tablet is the desk one step narrower.

- **Footer on a phone**: Home · ‹ page › · bell · +, 44px targets. Profile,
  Settings, sign-out and the theme move into the Go-to sheet (a bottom sheet,
  3-across grid). The owner's + is icon-only until `lg`.
- **Header**: title (34px) and page buttons share line one; filters take ONE
  line below, shrinking and truncating rather than wrapping.
- **Card row**: the two cards become **one swiped strip with dots**, equal
  height — keep each card compact so the pair stays ~200–240px and the list is
  on the first screen.
- **Rows**: task rows become `PhoneTaskRow`; a tap opens the quick-update sheet,
  not the full task. `RecordList` bleeds to both edges below `sm`.
- **Records**: one main action + ⋯ sheet; details open as full-height sheets.
  Columns stack and the page scrolls (`useFitFrame` only acts from `lg`).
- Chip rows and tab strips **scroll sideways**; they never wrap or widen the page.
- ⚠️ **Never branch RENDERED output on `useMediaQuery`** — the server cannot
  know the width and the difference is a hydration error. Show and hide with
  CSS (`hidden xl:grid`); the hook is for behaviour only.
- A list's fixed columns must leave the name column room at 375px and at `lg`
  (`gridFor()` in `record-list.tsx` floors it; still add up your widths and use
  `hideBelow` / `defaultHidden`).

## 11. Faces

Every person circle is **`PersonFace`** (`studio/face.tsx`, Blobatar): colour by
role, expression by their work (`lib/face-mood.ts`, from `/api/faces`, refreshed
every 5 min). Faces ≥36px follow the pointer; task-row faces "peek" (face, then
initials, in step). Staff see calm faces outside their own companies. Reduced
motion = still faces. Do not draw a plain initials circle.

**Rules from Desk that still hold:**
- **No bare `<select>`** and no `<datalist>`. `FluidSelect` / `SelectField` /
  `Combobox`; `Select` only where it already is.
- **No odd pixel sizes.** Desk components keep `text-xs/sm/base` (a pixel
  literal opts out of density); Studio code uses its own fixed scale (§3) and
  nothing in between. Set the size on a container so children cannot fall back
  to the browser's 16px.
- **One box per form.** Desk controls share `CONTROL_BOX`; inside a Studio form
  `.st-form` lifts them all to 40px together. Never mix heights in one column.
- **Hairlines separate; shadows only float** (menus, sheets, panels).
- **Colour carries meaning, not decoration.** In Studio the action colour is
  ink, not Desk's blue (`--accent` is redefined to ink); blue survives as
  information (`--st-blue`) and as the one button in emails.
- **Outline lucide icons, no emoji in chrome.**
- **Sorting and filtering are URLs**, replaced not pushed; search filters as you
  type (300ms).
- Legacy class names `.glass`, `.elevated`, `.vibrancy`, `.nav-frost`,
  `.glass-menu` still exist and resolve to a flat surface (about 47 files use
  them). Do not use them in new work.

## 13. Building a new page — checklist

1. Find its board in `design/studio-mockup/boards/` (and the `M_*`/`S_*` board
   for phone/staff). No board? Follow the nearest page's grammar and say so.
2. Server page gathers the data once; a client component under
   `components/studio/<area>/` does layout only. Cut data per viewer on the
   server (`guardOwner` / `guardViewer` / portal checks on every action).
3. Wrap it in `StudioScope` → `StudioHeader` → `StudioCardRow` of two
   `StudioCard`s → the list (`RecordList variant="studio"`) or the record.
4. Colours only from `--st-*` / `--sh-*`; sizes from §3; buttons from `stBtn`.
5. Filters through `useUrlFilters`; a search bar through `stFloatBar`.
6. Forms: `StudioSheet` or the record's Edit tab; `.st-form` + `.st-field`;
   pick-lists `StudioChoiceMenu`/`StudioMenu`; dates `DateInput` in `st-date`.
7. Tag pop-ups `data-st-menu`; portalled ones carry `studio`. Esc one layer.
8. Columns? `useFitFrame` from `lg`, lists scroll inside themselves.
9. Add the route to `nav.ts` (the footer and Go-to derive from it), and for
   staff to `staffStops()`; links via `useStudioPaths()`.
10. Check at 1440×900 against the board, at ~1000px, and at 393px — light and
    dark, reduced motion on. Footer top − floating bar bottom = 12px.
11. Update this file and `memory/studio_redesign.md` if a rule changed.
