Director · phone & tablet

The rules every page follows

Nothing is taken away — the same cards, words and order as on the desk. What does not fit a hand is reshaped, never hidden.

1
The footer fits a thumb
Phone: Home · ‹ page › · bell · +. Nothing falls off the edge any more (the + was cut off). Profile, sign out and light/dark move into the Go-to sheet, which opens from the page name.
2
The list comes first
The two dark summary cards become ONE card you swipe through (dots show there is a second). The first screen always reaches the list.
3
One tap target size
Every button, chip and row is at least 40px tall. Text never drops under 12px.
4
Filters scroll sideways
A row of chips slides left and right instead of wrapping into three lines.
5
Records: one main action
A task, person or company shows its main action as a button; the rest sit behind ⋯ in a sheet. Tabs scroll sideways under the header.
6
Details open as sheets
An Outbox item, a file, an event: on a phone they slide up full height with a back arrow; on a tablet they sit beside the list.
7
Search floats 10px above the footer
Same bar as the desk, full width minus the gutters, with the status chips sliding under it.
8
Tablet = desk, one step narrower
Portrait iPad keeps the two summary cards side by side (shorter), lists keep their key columns, and a record shows its details panel beside it. iPad landscape (1180 wide) already uses the desk layout.
