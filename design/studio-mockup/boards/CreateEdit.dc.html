Create & edit

One pattern, every record
01
One “+ New”, every record
The footer’s + New opens one card with a tab per record type. It already knows the page you are on — on People it opens on Person.
02
Quick first, full when needed
The card asks only what a record cannot exist without. “Open the full …” turns it into the record page itself, as an unsaved draft.
03
Adding and editing are one screen
The draft IS the record page: same Details, same controls, same places. Create it and it simply stops being a draft — nothing moves.
04
Edit where it is
Click a value to change it — a pick-list, a calendar, a people picker, a text box. Undo on every change. The full form only for many fields at once.
